Who we are

Serving Jain families across Greater Austin

A volunteer-run home for Jain philosophy, education, and community in Cedar Park & Greater Austin.

The Jain Sangh of Greater Austin (JSGA) is run entirely by volunteers from the Jain community in Cedar Park and the surrounding Austin area. We try to follow the core Jain principles of Ahimsa (non-violence), Anekantavada (a many-sided view of truth), and Aparigraha (non-attachment) in how we run things.

We gather for daily darshan, Paryushan and other festivals, Pathshala classes for our kids, and the seva projects that keep the Sangh and our Derasar running. A few years back we completed our Shilanyas and Khatmuhurat ceremony — an important step toward a permanent Derasar here in Central Texas.

This website, like everything else here, is put together by Sangh members. If something's out of date, let the board know.

For our kids

Pathshala — Jain education for the next generation

Pathshala is our weekly class for kids, taught by volunteers from the Sangh. Classes cover the Navkar Mantra, Jain stories and festivals, and how to practice ahimsa and compassion day to day.

What to expect

  • Age-grouped classes, taught by Sangh volunteers
  • Registration and membership dues are both handled in the same form
  • We can always use more teaching assistants — see the Volunteer Portal
